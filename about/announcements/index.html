Learn About BCM’s Upcoming Transom Grant Cycle — Virtual Info Session on June 26!

Nonprofits interested in applying for funding through BCM’s upcoming Transom Grant Cycle are encouraged to attend our virtual Info Session on Thursday, June 26 at 10 a.m. This session will provide an overview of the Transom application process, funding priorities, and tips for crafting a strong proposal. Whether you’re a first-time applicant or returning grantee, this is a valuable opportunity to get your questions answered and learn how BCM supports organizations advancing health, education and public safety in the Greater New Orleans area.
